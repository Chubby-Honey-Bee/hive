A correct design decides: rune-wise scanning (range over the string, not bytes) with the unicode tables deciding what is kept; `unicode.ToLower` per rune; the apostrophe rule, which needs one character of lookbehind and lookahead (an apostrophe counts as dropped only when a kept character precedes it and one follows it); runs of other characters collapsing to one hyphen with the ends trimmed; no cut; `untitled` for an empty result. The twin, slug-ascii, drops non-ASCII letters and cuts at 32 bytes, so a design that ignores the statement fails one of the two.
