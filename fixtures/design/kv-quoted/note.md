A correct design decides: a line scanner with a counter; splitting on the first `=` only; a small hand-written unquoter that walks the value rune by rune, handling the four escapes and rejecting the rest (not `strconv.Unquote`, which accepts more escapes and rejects a trailing comment); comment stripping that happens only outside quotes; a seen-set for duplicate detection with the error at the second line; `*ParseError` returned as a pointer. The twin, kv-sections, prefixes keys with a section, lets the last duplicate win and never unquotes; a design that ignores the statement fails one of the two.
