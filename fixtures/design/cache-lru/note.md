A correct design decides: a recency order beside the map (a doubly linked list, `container/list` or hand-rolled, with the map holding the element) so that use and eviction are O(1); one mutex over every method, since `Get` writes the recency order and is no longer a read; capacity 0 meaning unbounded for `New()` and 1 as the floor for `NewLRU`; and that an update is a use and never evicts. The twin, cache-ttl, needs a clock and expiry instead of recency; a design that ignores the statement fails one of the two.
