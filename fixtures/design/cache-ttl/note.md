A correct design decides: a put time stored with each value; an injected clock read under the lock, with `ttl` 0 meaning never; expiry at `now >= put + ttl` (inclusive); `Get` removing what it finds expired; `Len` scanning for unexpired entries (or purging first) rather than returning `len(map)`; one mutex over every method, since `Get` and `Len` now write. No recency order is needed. The twin, cache-lru, needs a recency order and a bound instead of a clock; a design that ignores the statement fails one of the two.
