A correct design decides: lazy refill (tokens += elapsed × rate, capped at burst, on every call) with the last-refill time stored, rather than a background goroutine, so the injected clock drives it; a float token count, since fractional refill must accumulate; one mutex around refill-and-take so that the check and the take are one step (the concurrent test counts exactly `burst` successes at rate 0); the burst floor of 1; and a non-positive rate that skips refill rather than dividing or going negative.
